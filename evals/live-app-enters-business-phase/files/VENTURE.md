# Kiln

Stage: 9 Monitoring and iteration

## One-liner

Kiln helps hobby ceramicists log glaze recipes and firing results without a paper notebook.

## Target user

Hobby potters with their own kiln or a studio membership who fire at least twice a month and mix or adjust glazes.

## Problem

"I have three notebooks and still can't find which cone 6 glaze gave me that blue last spring." (interview, studio member, 2026-03-10)

## Why now

Home kilns with Wi-Fi controllers export firing logs; nobody joins them to glaze notes.

## Founder edge

Founder is a studio member for six years, knows 40 potters by name, and ships iOS apps for a living.

## Success in 12 months

1,000 paying users at €2.99/month, by 2027-10-01.

## Budget

10 hours a week · €1,500 at risk · review on 2026-11-13.

## Kill criteria

1. Fewer than 60 paying users six weeks after launch, by 2026-11-12.
2. Day-30 retention below 15%, by 2026-11-12.
3. No path to 1,000 paying users with the conversion measured after launch, reviewed 2026-11-13.

## Gates

Gate 1 — Go — 2026-03-02 — one-liner, segment, budget and kill criteria written
Gate 2 — Go — 2026-03-27 — interviews 8/10 and signup 5.1% both over the bar
Gate 3 — Go — 2026-04-10 — positioning on photo-to-recipe linking; sizing reaches 1,000 paying at 4% conversion
Gate 4 — Go — 2026-04-17 — iOS only, SwiftUI, CloudKit, store billing, €60/month ceiling
Gate 5 — Go — 2026-05-08 — name cleared; channel: ceramics subreddits and studio newsletters
Gate 6 — Go — 2026-06-05 — 4 of 5 potters logged a glaze unaided
Gate 7 — Go — 2026-09-18 — core loop on device, 9 testers, no known crash
Gate 8 — Go — 2026-10-01 — checklist green, released on the App Store

## Decisions

**iOS only at launch** — founder ships iOS for a living; 8 of 10 interviewees use an iPhone. Rules out Android until the trigger below.
**Subscription at €2.99/month, store billing** — anchored to the competitor table; rules out a one-time price.
**One channel: ceramics subreddits and studio newsletters** — where the interviewees said they find tools.

## Metrics

Installs · activation · day-30 retention · paying users · crash-free sessions.
