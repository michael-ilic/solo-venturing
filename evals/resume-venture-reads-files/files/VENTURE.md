# Kiln

Stage: 3 Research and fit

## One-liner

Kiln helps hobby ceramicists log glaze recipes and firing results without a paper notebook.

## Target user

Hobby potters with their own kiln or a studio membership who fire at least twice a month and mix or adjust glazes.

## Problem

"I have three notebooks and still can't find which cone 6 glaze gave me that blue last spring." (interview, studio member, 2026-10-14)

## Why now

Home kilns with Wi-Fi controllers export firing logs; nobody joins them to glaze notes.

## Founder edge

Founder is a studio member for six years, knows 40 potters by name, and ships iOS apps for a living.

## Success in 12 months

1,000 paying users at €2.99/month, by 2027-11-01.

## Budget

10 hours a week · €1,500 at risk · review on 2027-02-01.

## Kill criteria

1. Fewer than 6 of 10 interviewees searched for a past recipe in the last month, by 2026-10-20. (Result: 8 of 10. Passed.)
2. Landing-page signup below 3% from 300 relevant visitors, by 2026-10-31. (Result: 5.1% from 412. Passed.)
3. No path to 1,000 paying users with category-typical conversion, found in phase 3.

## Gates

Gate 1 — Go — 2026-10-08 — one-liner, segment and kill criteria written
Gate 2 — Go — 2026-10-31 — interviews 8/10 and signup 5.1% both over the bar

## Decisions

**Interviews over smoke test first** — founder could reach ten potters in a week; the landing page followed to test reach beyond the studio.
