# Errors

A quality gate, not a diary. Read before work that resembles a past failure.

## Don't quote a market number without its source

Seen: 2026-10-09 — reported by founder
What happened: a "roughly 2 million hobby potters in the EU" figure was stated in chat with no source; the founder asked where it came from and it could not be traced.
Rule: every number that could shape a decision enters RESEARCH.md with a link or is labelled "assumption" before it is said aloud.
